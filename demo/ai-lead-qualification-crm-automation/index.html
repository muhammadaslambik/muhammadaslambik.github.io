01 · Project Overview

From an incoming lead to an automated business action.

This project demonstrates how AI automation can reduce manual lead screening and help businesses process incoming enquiries in a more structured and scalable way.

The Problem

Businesses can receive leads from multiple sources, and manually reading, evaluating, prioritizing, and recording every enquiry can take time and create inconsistent processes.

The Solution

Incoming lead data is sent to an automation workflow where AI helps analyze the enquiry, classify relevant information, update the lead record, and support follow-up actions.

02 · Workflow Architecture

How the automation flows.

The demo starts from a website form and passes the submitted data through the automation workflow.

STEP 01Lead FormVisitor submits information and requirements.
STEP 02n8n WebhookWorkflow receives data from the website.
STEP 03AI AnalysisLead enquiry is analyzed and structured.
STEP 04CRM / DataProcessed lead can be stored automatically.
STEP 05Gmail ActionNotification or follow-up can be triggered.
